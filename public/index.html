How it works

01

Enable replays

In Fortnite: Settings → Game → Replays → Record Replays → On.

02

Open the companion

Run NBFC TRACKER on Windows. It reads your saved replays and keeps profiles separate.

03

Bring it to your stream

Copy the overlay URL into an OBS Browser Source. Keep the companion running.

Important

⌂

Your replays. Your PC.

Reads saved replay files. No game memory access or Epic login required. Your replay data stays on this PC.
