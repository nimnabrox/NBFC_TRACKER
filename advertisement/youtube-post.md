I made a free, open-source Fortnite chest tracker 🧰

NBFC TRACKER automatically counts opened chests from your saved replays. Once the companion is running, new chests update automatically — no manual counter clicks or repeated total updates.

• Track each player profile and review match history.
• Add a clean OBS counter to your stream.
• Change the overlay label, last-match display or language without replacing its URL.
• Choose from 18 languages.
• Replay data stays on your PC.

Download for Windows: https://nbfct.nimnabro.com
Source code: https://github.com/nimnabrox/NBFC_TRACKER

Fortnite only keeps limited replay history. If you want to include older chests, you can enter an optional starting milestone once. Future chests are added automatically. Live replay analysis updates periodically, so it is not instant.

Keep OBS and the companion running on the same PC. Enable replay recording in Fortnite first.
